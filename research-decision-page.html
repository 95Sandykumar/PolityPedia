Phase 1 research · 4 Nov 2026 · India · Political Science

PolityPedia: what to build first

A student web app for Political Science aspirants preparing for UGC NET, CUET PG and TGT/PGT. The teacher partner stays private.

Verdict

Do not clone the reference app. It is a generic AppX white-label tutor app (attendance, fees, homework). AppX already sells that.

Build V1 as a free "one subject, three exams" hub. Syllabus explorer, PYQ bank tagged by exam, daily quiz, mocks, AI tutor that answers only from the teacher's notes, and a weekly AI-drafted newsletter. Paid courses come in V2.

Deadline that matters: UGC NET December 2026 cycle and CUET PG 2027 registration. Aim to go live by mid-December 2026.

Part V

What goes in each version

V1 · Free hub · recommended now
  • Pick your exam on sign-up (phone OTP or Google)
  • Syllabus explorer: 10 units, topic pages
  • PYQ bank tagged by unit, exam, year, with explanations
  • Daily 10-question quiz + streak
  • Topic tests and full mocks in an NTA-style screen
  • Weak-unit heatmap + score vs last cut-off
  • AI tutor that answers only from teacher notes and shows its source
  • Teacher video index (links out to YouTube)
  • Weekly newsletter signup + archive
  • Hindi / English toggle, works well on cheap phones
V2 · Paid
  • Paid courses and notes PDFs (watermarked)
  • Razorpay payments, UPI
  • Live classes (YouTube unlisted or Zoom)
  • Doubt forum
  • Teacher admin panel to upload content
V3 · Scale
  • Install as phone app (PWA, then Play Store wrap)
  • Test series for state PGT exams
  • Community and leaderboards
  • Second subject, same engine
Part VI

Newsletter: "Pol Sci Weekly"

  • Sent every Sunday. AI drafts it. Teacher reviews it in about 15 minutes.
  • 5 news items, each mapped to a syllabus unit ("Why this matters for Unit 6").
  • 1 thinker of the week, 10 MCQs, 1 PYQ trend, upcoming exam dates.
  • Each issue also lives on the site as a page, so it brings Google traffic.
  • Gap: no competitor sends a Pol Sci exam newsletter. PW only does a YouTube weekly.
Part VII

Risks

High

The teacher works for Adda247. A paid rival product may break his contract. This is likely why he stays anonymous. Check his contract before V2 goes paid.

High

His videos belong to the Adda247 channel. Only link to public YouTube videos. Never download or re-host them.

Med

AI tutor can give wrong answers. Ground it on teacher notes only, show the source, and let students flag errors.

Med

NTA question papers are public, but explanations must be our own writing.

Med

TGT is a weak fit. Most TGT exams test full Social Science, not only Pol Sci.
