## 4. Opportunities (retention ideas, one line each)

1. Make "Continue" the first card on Home: open attempt, question number, one tap to resume.
2. Add a "Today" card: 10 questions, a progress ring and a streak flame, tied to a daily goal Arjun sets in Me.
3. Turn Saved into "Revise": a Practise button that plays the saved questions as one session with answer and explanation shown after each.
4. Spaced repetition on Wrong: re-surface each wrong question after 1, 3 and 7 days, and show "5 due today" on Home and as a badge on the Saved tab.
5. Finish screen v2: show right and wrong counts, the wrong list, "Practise these wrong ones", and the delta against the last attempt.
6. Progress tab or a Me section: tests taken, accuracy trend, questions per day, a 14-day activity strip.
7. Topic catalogue plus weak-topic chips: "Weak: Federalism 40 percent, practise 10".
8. Real reminders: installable web app (manifest and service worker), one daily push at his study hour, and an opt-in WhatsApp or email nudge.
9. Teacher notice becomes a deep link (open the named mock or video) with an unread dot on the Home tab.
10. Exam countdown tied to HIS exam, with a "days left, questions per day needed" line to make the number actionable.
11. Revision as a place: a "Revision (AI)" entry from Saved and from the wrong-answer list, with the audio bar for the commute, and generate audio on first read.
12. Show a clear error state ("could not load, try again") instead of an empty state when a read fails, plus an alert when a list read returns 400.
13. Pin the question to a visible action: after a peek (Reveal), offer "Add to Marked" so peeks also reach revision.
14. Weekly summary notice from the app itself (hours, questions, best topic) so Home has fresh content even when the teacher posts nothing.
